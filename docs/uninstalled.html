Nitpost was removed from Chrome.

Removing the extension cleared its local storage. Your drafts, their screenshots and reference images, recent folders, destination preferences and send records are gone from this Chrome profile.

Remove the helper too

The helper is still on your Mac. This command removes it, its registration with Chrome and the screenshot files it saved.

"$HOME/Library/Application Support/Nitpost/nitpost" register uninstall

Then delete the installed Nitpost folder, ~/Library/Application Support/Nitpost. The installer never edits the Chrome profile database, agent configuration or transcripts.
